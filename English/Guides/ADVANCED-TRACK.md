# Advanced Track

Begin the advanced track only after you can explain networking, HTTP, Linux/Windows process basics, identity fundamentals and safe lab methodology.

## Research foundations

Start with Modules **061–068** for research methodology, CPU/syscalls, assembly, executable formats, debugging, memory-corruption mechanics, reverse engineering and fuzzing.

## Advanced standard

A strong advanced lab has a hypothesis, controlled environment, minimal reproducer, instrumentation, evidence, root-cause explanation, defensive implication and regression test. Do not treat a crash, scanner result or suspicious event as the end of the investigation.

Use toy programs and intentionally vulnerable targets for exploit-research concepts. The objective is to understand corruption, parser state and mitigations—not to build weaponized payloads for real systems.
